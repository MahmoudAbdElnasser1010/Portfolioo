Overview

Solace's storefront was converting well below category benchmarks, and the checkout flow — spread across five separate pages — was the biggest suspect.

I led the redesign of the storefront, product pages, and checkout, and laid the groundwork for a component library the marketing team could reuse for campaigns.

The Challenge

The existing site was fast to browse but slow to buy — every added click in checkout was measurably losing sales, and the visual design didn't match the quality of the product itself.

  • Five-step checkout with a 68% abandonment rate.
  • Product pages reused a generic template that undersold hero products.
  • No shared component library, so every campaign page was built from scratch.

The Approach

I mapped the full purchase funnel and worked with the founders to compress checkout into a single page with inline validation. Product pages were rebuilt around large imagery and social proof placed where hesitation was highest.

  • Combined shipping, payment, and review into one scroll-based checkout page.
  • Added a sticky add-to-cart bar with real-time stock and shipping estimates.
  • Shipped a Webflow component library so campaigns launched in days, not weeks.
