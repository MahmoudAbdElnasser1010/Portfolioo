Overview

Halo Systems was rebranding ahead of a Series A, and needed an identity that could stretch from the product UI to investor decks without losing coherence.

I led the visual identity work and then built the front-end component library that made the new brand usable by every team, not just design.

The Challenge

The old brand had no real system behind it — every team was recreating buttons, colors, and layouts slightly differently, and it showed in inconsistent decks and a dated product UI.

  • No documented color, type, or spacing system.
  • Marketing and product used two different visual languages.
  • Every new deck or one-pager started from a blank file.

The Approach

I designed a compact but flexible identity system — a refined mark, a restrained color palette, and a type scale — then translated it directly into a Storybook component library the whole company could pull from.

  • Defined core tokens for color, type, spacing, and motion.
  • Built a shared component library in Storybook, wired into the product codebase.
  • Created reusable deck and one-pager templates for the whole team.
